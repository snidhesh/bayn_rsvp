What Bayn is

4.8 million square metres of coast that belongs to neither city.

Ghantoot sits on the border, on the last stretch of undeveloped beachfront in the Dubai to Abu Dhabi corridor. ORA's first UAE project takes 4.8 million sqm of it and gives more than half back as open space: a million square metres of parks, seven kilometres of waterfront, a marina, lagoons and a 100,000 sqm sports club.

The first release is 464 homes across two gated clusters, three to five bedrooms, ten typologies, handover in 2028. Launch day pricing and allocation apply to the open house only.
